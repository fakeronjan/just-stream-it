Rebrand Roundup

Four teams enter 2026 under a new name, logo, or both

→
Baker's Dirty Dozen → The Aristocrats
Karl Abel

Baker's Dirty Dozen finished 9th at 5-9 last year, and Karl didn't tinker, he detonated it: zero keepers, the only true rebuild in the league. Going from "Dirty" to "Aristocrats" for a team blowing up everything it owns is either very on-brand or exactly backwards - your call.

→
Daddy Nielsen → Olave Oyl
Joe Sebranek

This isn't a team in crisis rebranding. Daddy Nielsen went 10-4 last year, good for 5th, and Joe tore up the name anyway, just to hand the team over to his own keeper. Olave Oyl is a good team having fun, not a bad one starting over.

→
Keep 'em and weep → Rice Rice Baby
Kris Wikstrom

Keep 'em and weep finished 2-12, dead last in the league, and honestly the old name was foreshadowing. Rice Rice Baby trades the tears for an actual receiver - for once, the joke's on offense.

→
McConkey Kong Country → McConkey Kong Country 2
Ronjan Sikdar

McConkey Kong Country put up 1909.7 points last year, third-most in the league, and still finished 7th at 6-8, one spot outside the playoffs. That's exactly the setup a sequel is for: give the first one the ending it actually deserved. Fittingly, he re-drafted his namesake, Ladd McConkey, in round four.
