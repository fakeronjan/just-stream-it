Draft Night

The League's First Keeper Draft Is in the Books

September 1, 2026 · 12 owners, 19 rounds, 228 picks

1.01 · The Aristocrats

Karl Abel is the only owner who kept nobody, walked away from a 9th-place finish, and rebuilt from scratch under a new name. He opened with three running backs in his first four picks (Bijan Robinson at 1.01, then Ashton Jeanty and Josh Jacobs), ran four straight wide receivers from picks five through eight, then paired Travis Kelce and Hunter Henry back to back at tight end. Position by position, not player by player.

1.02 · Keep 'em and weep

Kris Wikstrom is coming off the league's worst record, 2-12, and used the top of the board to reload: Lamar Jackson at 1.02, then Quinshon Judkins (kept for a round-9 cost) and Tyler Shough (kept for round 13, one of the two best-value keepers in the league). He also landed running back Woody Marks 61 picks past his ADP in round 15, the deepest fall at the position all night.

1.03 · Mahomes is where the heart is

Lisette Bruens kept Christian McCaffrey and Bo Nix, and the Nix keeper (a round-two cost) is the one real overpay in the league by the model's math. Her first live pick after that wasn't a reach, it was fate: a team named for Patrick Mahomes actually landed Patrick Mahomes, at pick 27. She's one of five owners now carrying four quarterbacks.

1.04 · McConkey Kong Country 2

Ronjan Sikdar's team picked up a sequel in the name and kept its running back: Jahmyr Gibbs at the round-one cost, plus Trevor Lawrence for round five. He built out to four quarterbacks, including a round-11 flier on Aaron Rodgers, and closed heavy at wide receiver, four of his last picks at the position, landing Tre Tucker and Jalen Nailor well past their ADP.

1.05 · Conquering Heroes

Ben Porter's three keepers, Kenneth Walker III, Emeka Egbuka and Colston Loveland, all graded as fair-to-good value. His boldest live pick was MarShawn Lloyd in round six, 57 picks ahead of ADP and the single biggest reach of the entire draft.

1.06 · Olave Oyl

Joe Sebranek's rebrand from Daddy Nielsen tells you the plan: he kept Chris Olave, then opened round one with Jalen Hurts, part of a three-quarterback run at picks six through eight. All three of his keepers, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Olave and Javonte Williams, landed as value by the model, and he doubled down late with reaches on Gunnar Helm and Malik Davis.

1.07 · Mednis Madness

Mariss Mednis's first pick was the third quarterback in that round-one run, Caleb Williams, taken 15 slots ahead of his ADP. She kept Cam Skattebo and Wan'Dale Robinson, both solid value, and added a round-six reach on tight end Isaiah Likely.

1.08 · Illusions Michael

Pete Gallagher kept Josh Allen at a fair cost and never drafted another quarterback, the only 19-round roster in the league with just one. He poured the savings into wide receiver instead, eight of them, and found value everywhere late: Keaton Mitchell fell 66 picks past ADP in round 17.

1.09 · Lamar and Drake's Grahamy's

Imran Hirani is back after finishing runner-up, and his three keepers, Daniel Jones, Kyle Pitts Sr. and Luther Burden III (the second-best keeper value in the league), came cheap. He clearly wanted quarterback depth beyond that: Fernando Mendoza and Carson Beck were both rookie-QB reaches, 50 and 66 picks ahead of their ADP.

1.10 · Team Kantastrophe

Cara Kantrowitz is the defending champion, and her keepers read like a title defense: Jonathan Taylor at essentially fair cost, Drake Maye, and Matthew Stafford kept for a round-10 cost, the third-best keeper value in the league. She closed with a round-16 flier on Chris Rodriguez Jr., 62 picks past his ADP.

1.11 · Buffalo Abusement Park

Melinda Thomas kept Ja'Marr Chase, the second-priciest keeper in the league behind only Bo Nix, and James Cook III alongside him. She stayed light at quarterback, just two all draft, and grabbed running back Jonah Coleman 67 picks past ADP in round 17, the biggest fall of the night.

1.12 · Terrible Towels

Mike Higgins finished last a year ago and answered with the best-value keeper duo in the league: Jayden Daniels at a fair round-one cost, then Jaxson Dart for a round-12 cost, the single biggest bargain of any keeper in Just Stream It. He added Shedeur Sanders as a round-16 quarterback flier behind them.
