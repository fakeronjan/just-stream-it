Draft Night

The League's First Keeper Draft Is in the Books

September 1, 2026 · 12 owners, 19 rounds, 228 picks

The Rebuild

Karlos Abel walked in with zero keepers, the only owner in the league who protected nobody, and walked out with a new name: The Aristocrats. He opened with three running backs in his first four picks, ran four straight wide receivers from picks five through eight, then paired Travis Kelce and Hunter Henry back to back at tight end. Position by position, not player by player.

Arms Race

Five owners now carry four quarterbacks apiece: Lisette, Ronjan, Ben, Imran, and Cara. Pete Gallagher took the other side of that bet. His keeper Josh Allen is the only quarterback on his entire 19-round roster, backed instead by eight wide receivers.

Keeper Ledger

Twenty-seven players came back on keeper slots, enough to fill five of round one's twelve picks. Prices mostly landed fair. The one real overpay by the model's math was Lisette's Bo Nix, kept at a round-two cost. The best value in the league belongs to Michael Higgins, who anchored his room with keeper Jayden Daniels and then kept Jaxson Dart for a round-12 cost, the single biggest discount to market of any player kept this year.

Rebrands

Three teams showed up under new banners. Daddy Nielsen became Olave Oyl (Joseph Sebranek kept Chris Olave, so it tracks), Baker's Dirty Dozen became The Aristocrats, and McConkey Kong Country got a sequel: McConkey Kong Country 2.

Running the Board

Round one had its own run: Jalen Hurts, Caleb Williams, and keeper Josh Allen went back to back to back at picks six through eight. Wide receiver runs broke out again and again, six separate times over the course of the draft. Kickers, unsurprisingly, waited: 12 of the league's 17 kickers came off the board in the final three rounds, three of them in a row to close out round 19.
