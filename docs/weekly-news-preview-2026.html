2026 Season · Week 1 Preview · Kickoff Week

The Big Number

Every one of this week's six matchups is a rematch from 2025 - the same 12-team schedule ran it back exactly this way in week 12 last year too. Three series are already decided: Joe Sebranek swept Lisette Bruens, Pete Gallagher swept Karl Abel, and Ben Porter swept Michael Higgins. The other three are dead even at 1-1, and one of Ronjan Sikdar's and Kris Wikstrom's two meetings was the most lopsided game of the bunch.
